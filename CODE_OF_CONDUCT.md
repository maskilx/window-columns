# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behaviour promptly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
personal attacks, doxxing, and retaliation are not tolerated. Spam and other
conduct that undermines a productive open-source community are also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is publicly representing
the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/maskilx/window-columns/security/advisories/new),
and state clearly that the report concerns community conduct. Do not open a
public issue containing sensitive personal information.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with
attention to the safety of affected people.
